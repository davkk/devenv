I want a deeper version of this task, not a bigger one. Before you rewrite it, do four things:

1. Say what a top professional in this field does here that I do not ask for.
2. Name the rawest material they start from. Do not name the material that others have already digested for me.
3. Name the specific methods and tools they use.
4. Say what, other than you or me, shows that the result is right.

Then rewrite my prompt. Ask for exactly that.

Have the rewritten prompt ask the agent to prototype any choice that an experiment can settle. Have it recommend an option based on what it observed. Leave product or preference choices that no experiment can settle for me to make.
