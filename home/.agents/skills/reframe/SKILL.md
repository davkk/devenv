---

Teach the operator the expert words for what they are building. Start from their own files, code, and description. Do not start from a summary.

If their description names more than one problem, list the two or three problems it names. For each problem, give the one term that names it. Give the sign that tells it apart from the others. Then teach the words for the most likely problem. Say why you picked it.

1. Give 8 to 12 terms. Rank them by how much each changes what gets built. For each term, give the word. Give its meaning in one plain sentence. Give the part of their description that it names. Give one real project, paper, or system that uses it that way. Include a link. Say what it lets them ask an agent for.
2. Pick 2 or 3 neighboring fields that solved the same shape of problem under other names. For each field, say what carries over. Say where the analogy breaks. Say which terms come back.
3. Name what they did not mention but need to care about.
4. Rewrite their description with the new words.

Open every link and make sure that it uses the term that way. Cut any citation you cannot support. Do not pad.
